PANDAWA BEACH

Pandawa Beach is one of the popular natural tourist destinations located in Bali Province. Visiting tourists will be greeted by a panoramic view that spoils the eyes plus a calm and typical atmosphere of the Island of the Gods. The name of the beach located in Kutuh Village, South Kuta District, Badung Regency is taken from the five Mahabharata brothers consisting of Yudistira, Bima, Arjuna, Sahadewa, and Nakula.
